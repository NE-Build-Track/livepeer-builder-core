| D52 | **Paid failover to another orchestrator, and `billable`** (John, 27 October; capped by D55, after [livepeer-python-gateway#83](https://github.com/livepeer/livepeer-python-gateway/pull/83)). | D54 | **The manifest is stored before signing** (Mike, 28 October; the follow-up to #61, superseding #56 and the stored-intent part of NE livepeer-python-gateway#7). Amends D53 (1) and D39. (2) `payment_state` (migration `0006`): `unknown` until the signer answers, `paid` on a validated reply, `unpaid` on a refusal (400, 401, 402, 403, 481, a 480 refresh, `SkipPaymentCycle`). `paid` is final; rows from before `0006` are `paid`.
