| D57 | **Repeat-safe commands: the final job rules and the first defects** (Mike, 29 October, ISS-11 [#24](https://github.com/Cloud-SPE/Network-Engineering-SPE/issues/24) and ISS-14 [#27](https://github.com/Cloud-SPE/Network-Engineering-SPE/issues/27), from the idempotency guide `docs/design-docs/m3-guides/iss-11-14-idempotency.md`; Q1–Q5 locked; slices 3 and 4 will amend it). (4) **Job rules (Part A), final; replaces D11's interim 409.** A repeat with another fingerprint is 409 `OperationConflict`.
